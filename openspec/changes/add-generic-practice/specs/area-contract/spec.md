# Spec Delta

## Purpose

The area contract is what every practice area (Vocabulary, Kanji, Grammar and any later one) supplies to the shared practice module: its name and its questions for a set of JLPT levels, grouped by entry. The practice module only sees questions, never an area's own words, kanji or verbs.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Areas supply entries of questions for chosen levels
An area SHALL supply, for a given set of JLPT levels (one or more of N5, N4, N3, N2, N1), a list of entries. Each entry SHALL carry its own JLPT levels and its questions. Questions generated by the area and questions written by hand on an entry SHALL be supplied in the same form, so the practice module cannot tell them apart.

#### Scenario: Entries for a level
- **WHEN** the test-only Sample area is asked for its entries at N3
- **THEN** it supplies exactly the entries that carry N3, each with at least one question

#### Scenario: Entries carry their levels
- **WHEN** the Sample area is asked for its entries at N5 and N4
- **THEN** every entry supplied carries N5 or N4 among its levels

### Requirement: What a question carries
Every question SHALL carry a prompt text, one or more accepted answers, the Japanese texts the question already shows (possibly none), and an exercise kind. The kind SHALL be either a Japanese answer or a meaning in plain English, and it decides how the answer is checked.

#### Scenario: A Japanese question
- **WHEN** the Sample area supplies its question about reading `高い`
- **THEN** the question has a prompt, the accepted answer `たかい`, the shown text `高い`, and the Japanese kind

#### Scenario: A meaning question
- **WHEN** the Sample area supplies its question about the meaning of `高い`
- **THEN** the question has the accepted answers `high`, `tall` and `expensive`, shows no Japanese text to exclude, and has the meaning kind

### Requirement: An area without questions is reported as empty
An area SHALL be reported as having questions exactly when, over all five JLPT levels, it supplies at least one entry that has at least one level and at least one question. An area that is reported empty SHALL show the "No questions in this area yet" screen of area selection when chosen.

#### Scenario: An area that supplies nothing
- **WHEN** an area supplies no entries
- **THEN** it is reported as having no questions

#### Scenario: An area with only empty entries
- **WHEN** an area supplies entries that have no questions
- **THEN** it is reported as having no questions

#### Scenario: The Sample area
- **WHEN** the Sample area is checked for questions
- **THEN** it is reported as having questions

#### Scenario: Every real area agrees with its questions
- **WHEN** each area the app lists is checked
- **THEN** it is reported as having questions exactly when it supplies an entry with a level and a question at the five levels

### Requirement: Adding an area means listing it
An area that implements the contract SHALL be practisable from start to finish once it is added to the list of areas, without any change to the practice module, the level picker, the question screen or the feedback screen.

#### Scenario: The Sample area is practised
- **WHEN** the app's list of areas is the Sample area alone and the user presses Enter on Welcome, Enter on area selection, Enter on the level picker
- **THEN** a question from the Sample area is shown with an answer field
